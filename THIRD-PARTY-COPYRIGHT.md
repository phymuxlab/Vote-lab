# Third-party, embed and image policy

- No advertising or analytics embed is added by this update.
- Supabase is a required infrastructure dependency.
- Google OAuth may be enabled through Supabase Auth if the project owner configures it.
- Organisation and nominee images are user-supplied content. The uploader is responsible for having permission to use them.
- The new VoteLab logo in `public/votelab-logo.svg` is included as project-owned source artwork.
- Do not add stock images, fonts, videos or logos to the production build without confirming the relevant licence or permission.
